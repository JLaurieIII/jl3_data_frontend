Why Mahomes Keeps Winning

When pressured, Mahomes completes 67.3% of his passes. League average? 42.1%. That's not luck. That's a quarterback who sees the field differently — and the data proves it. His average time to throw under pressure is 2.4 seconds, buying just enough time to find the open man.

The Eagles' Run Game Secret

Saquon averages 5.2 yards after contact. That means even when the defense does everything right, he's still gaining five yards. His expected yards based on blocking? Only 2.8. The difference is +2.4 yards of pure talent per carry.

Red Zone: Where Games Are Won

The Chiefs convert 71% of red zone trips into touchdowns. The Eagles? 64%. That 7% gap might seem small, but over a season it's the difference between a wild card and the #1 seed. Tonight, whichever team owns the red zone owns the Lombardi.
