Jalen Hurts: Super Bowl LIX MVP Stats

The Most Accurate Super Bowl QB Ever

Jalen Hurts completed 17 of 22 passes (77.3%) for 221 yards and 2 touchdowns. His career Super Bowl completion percentage of 73.3% is the highest in Super Bowl history (minimum 50 attempts).

Rushing Record: 72 Yards

Hurts rushed for 72 yards on 11 carries, breaking his own Super Bowl record for rushing yards by a quarterback (70 yards in Super Bowl LVII). He became the first QB in Super Bowl history to lead both teams in rushing since Steve Young in Super Bowl XXIX.

Postseason Rushing TD Record

With his rushing touchdown against the Chiefs, Hurts became the first quarterback in NFL history with 10 career rushing TDs in the postseason. His passer rating: 119.7.

Eagles Defense Dominates: 6 Sacks, 3 Turnovers

Cooper DeJean's Birthday Pick-Six

Rookie cornerback Cooper DeJean intercepted Patrick Mahomes and returned it 38 yards for a touchdown on his 22nd birthday. He became the first player in Super Bowl history to score on his birthday and only the second rookie to return an interception for a TD in a Super Bowl.

Chiefs First Half Disaster: 23 Yards

The Chiefs gained just 23 total yards in the first half — the second-lowest first-half yardage in Super Bowl history. Mahomes was sacked 6 times (a career high), all without the Eagles blitzing.

Three-Peat Denied

The Eagles defense ended Kansas City's historic bid to become the first team to win 3 consecutive Super Bowls. Mahomes finished with 3 turnovers including the DeJean pick-six.

Saquon Barkley: Record-Breaking Season

All-Time Single Season Rushing Record

While Saquon had a quiet Super Bowl (57 rushing yards on 25 carries), his season was historic. He set the NFL record for most rushing yards in a season: 2,504 yards. Combined with postseason, his total of 2,857 scrimmage yards surpassed Terrell Davis's record.

Birthday Championship

The Super Bowl fell on Saquon's 28th birthday. In his first season with the Eagles after leaving the Giants, he won his first championship and set multiple NFL records. Not a bad birthday gift.

Kendrick Lamar Halftime Show: Most-Watched Ever

Kendrick Lamar's Apple Music Super Bowl Halftime Show at Caesars Superdome in New Orleans became the most-watched halftime performance in television history. SZA joined as special guest, and Samuel L. Jackson appeared as Uncle Sam throughout the performance.
