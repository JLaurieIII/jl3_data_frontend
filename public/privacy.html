Overview

This privacy policy explains how James J. Laurie III Data Engineering Consulting ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or engage my services.

I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting your personal data. This policy will inform you about how I look after your personal data and tell you about your privacy rights.

Information I Collect

Information you provide directly

When you fill out the contact form or book a call, I collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Project details you share
  • Any other information you choose to provide

Information collected automatically

When you visit this website, I may collect:

I use the information I collect to:
