About

Why I do this work

I love data. I love finding insights hidden in it. And I love building systems that make those insights accessible to everyone—not just data scientists.

The work I actually do

I build data systems you can maintain. Not black boxes. Not dependencies. Clean infrastructure with proper handoff.

What drives me

I'm passionate about three things:

  • Data that reveals insights - I built a sports betting analytics platform using machine learning to find betting advantages in sports data. This is where my love for data started.
  • Systems that scale - I spent 3+ years at Charter Schools USA building every data pipeline the organization used. During that time, we grew from 82 to 104 schools—22 new schools relying on systems I built.
  • Clean, maintainable infrastructure - I don't overcomplicate things. If your team can't maintain it, I didn't build it right.

My philosophy

Most consultants build systems that require them. I build systems that don't.

Your ops team, your analysts, your marketing folks—they should be able to use and maintain what I build. That means clear documentation, sensible architecture, and training that actually sticks.

What makes me different

I've been burned by broken pipelines

I know what it's like when data breaks at 3am. I know what it costs when dashboards show stale data. I build monitoring and tests because I've lived without them.

I build for handoff, not dependency

Documentation isn't an afterthought—it's a core deliverable. Runbooks, architecture diagrams, troubleshooting guides, data dictionaries. Your team should be self-sufficient.

I optimize for cost efficiency

I won't push you to Snowflake if Postgres can handle your scale. I right-size compute, use incremental loads, and design for growth without waste.

I say "no" to bad-fit projects

If your problem doesn't need custom data infrastructure, I'll tell you. I'm not here to sell you things you don't need.
