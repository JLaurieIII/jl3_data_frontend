## Expanded Project Narrative — HubSpot Integration for Charter Schools

### Business Context (Expanded)

The charter school organization relied on HubSpot to manage:
- Prospective student leads
- Parent inquiries
- Application lifecycle stages
- Enrollment decisions

However, leadership lacked **clear visibility** into:
- Untouched or stalled leads
- Funnel drop-off points
- Application-to-enrollment conversion rates
- Performance across schools and regions

Reporting inside HubSpot alone was insufficient for:
- Multi-school aggregation
- Historical trend analysis
- Executive-level dashboards

---

## What Was Built (Expanded Detail)

### HubSpot Data Integration

I implemented a **HubSpot API-based data integration** that extracted:

- Contacts (students, parents, guardians)
- Associations between contacts and schools
- Custom objects representing:
  - Applications
  - Enrollment status
  - School-specific attributes
- Lifecycle stage changes
- Timestamped status transitions

This data was:
- Ingested programmatically (no CSV exports)
- Loaded incrementally
- Validated for schema drift
- Modeled for analytics use cases

---

### Analytics & Dashboard Enablement (Power BI)

Using the analytics-ready data layer, the organization was able to build **Power BI dashboards** that surfaced:

#### Enrollment Funnel Visibility
- Total leads by school
- Leads contacted vs untouched
- Applications started vs completed
- Enrollments confirmed vs dropped

#### Marketing Performance Insights
- Lead sources by channel
- Conversion rates by campaign
- Time-to-contact metrics
- School-level performance comparisons

These dashboards consumed **custom HubSpot objects**, which is not achievable with out-of-the-box HubSpot reporting alone.

## Realistic Business Impact (Explicit Numbers)

⚠️ **IMPORTANT FOR AI AGENT**  
The following statistics should be written as **illustrative but realistic outcomes**, framed as “data-informed estimates” rather than absolute guarantees.

### Lead Management Improvements

Within the first academic cycle after dashboard rollout:

- **22% reduction in untouched leads**
  - Previously hidden leads were identified and reassigned
- **18% faster average time-to-first-contact**
  - From ~4.5 days → ~3.7 days
- **14% increase in application completion rate**
  - Driven by follow-up prioritization

---

### Enrollment Impact (Modeled Outcome)

Based on historical enrollment conversion rates:

- **~6–9% increase in enrolled students**
  - Attributed to better lead visibility and follow-up
- Example framing:
  - “For a school enrolling ~400 students annually, this represented an estimated 25–35 additional enrollments.”

This should be framed as:
> “Improved operational efficiency and visibility translated into measurable enrollment gains.”

---

### Executive Visibility

Leadership gained:
- Real-time enrollment funnel snapshots
- Weekly performance comparisons across schools
- Early warnings for underperforming campaigns

This shifted decision-making from **reactive** to **proactive**.
